12 Factors of Knowledge Capitalism

12 Factors of Knowledge Capitalism

A framework for how knowledge creates, compounds, and captures value in networked systems.

Begin with Factor 1 twelve factors · read in sequence · ~60 minutes

Beneath every factor lies a single objective: survival under uncertainty — the maintenance of organization against entropy. Knowledge exists in service of action, and action in service of persistence.

In brief
  • The objective is survival under uncertainty: systems persist by converting what they know into better decisions. Factor 1 sets this standard.
  • The method is knowledge activation — the conversion of what is known into effective action wherever it is needed, through the interfaces of evolution, agency, and network.
  • The claim is that knowledge itself becomes the primary form of capital — cognitive capital (Factor 9) — and civilization's task is designing the mechanisms that maximize its liquidity (Factor 12).

Inspired by the original 12-Factor App and 12-Factor Agents.

The 12 Factors

I. Foundations

what knowledge is, and how it is carried

01Knowledge is Negative Entropy
Knowledge is not mere information but information that enables effective action. 02Language Compresses and Carries Knowledge
Among all the vessels humanity has invented to carry knowledge, language remains the most versatile.

II. The Evolution Interface

how conjecture and criticism create it

03Exploration Creates New Conjectures
Knowledge is not deduced but created, beginning as a bold and creative guess at how reality works. 04Criticism Hardens Conjecture into Knowledge
A conjecture is not yet knowledge. It earns that status only by surviving criticism.

III. The Agency Interface

how it converts into action

05Action is the Only Causal Chain to Survival
Agency is the groundwork on which knowledge earns its consequence. 06Cognitive Actors Are the Nodes of Agency
A cognitive actor is any system, carbon or silicon, that turns signals into actions toward an objective.

IV. The Network Interface

how it moves between actors

07Transmission Adds Context and Noise to the Core Message
Knowledge cannot traverse networks without context, and transmission never arrives clean. 08Shared Structures Enable Coordination at Scale
Coordination needs shared belief systems and the structures that sustain them.

V. Knowledge Capitalism

how networks score, trade, and design for it

09Capital is a Path-Dependent Scoring System
Once networked actors share structures, they begin to score, and that scoring is capital. 10The Market is a Knowledge-Utilization Machine
A market is not merely a place to trade. It is a machine for utilizing knowledge no single mind holds. 11Coordination Mechanisms Scale Civilization
How an entire civilization puts its dispersed knowledge to work: coordination mechanisms. 12Mechanism Design and the Tools to Maximize Welfare
Welfare does not arise on its own. It must be engineered, and a growing toolkit makes it possible.

Read sequentially — each factor builds on the last, and the twelfth closes the loop.
