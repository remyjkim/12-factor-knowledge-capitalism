Knowledge is not deduced but *created*. New knowledge begins as a conjecture, a bold and creative guess at how some piece of reality works, invented rather than derived. [Good explanations](https://www.thebeginningofinfinity.com/) are authored, not read off the world. Conjectures need raw material, so the cognitive actor must go out to meet the unknown, [inquiring](https://unitus.org/FULL/DewLog38.pdf) and exploring to surface the problems worth explaining. This act is sovereign, since no one can expand your realm of coverage for you. The [explore–exploit tradeoff](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) names its price. Effort spent exploring is withheld from exploiting what you already know, yet without it the frontier never moves. Tools extend the seeker's reach, from the [memex](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/1945/07/as-we-may-think/303881/) to [augmenting human intellect](https://www.dougengelbart.org/pubs/papers/scanned/Doug_Engelbart-AugmentingHumanIntellect.pdf). As artificial cognitive systems pursue [open-ended learning](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/Era-of-Experience%20/The%20Era%20of%20Experience%20Paper.pdf), exploration becomes their engine for [novel experience](https://yidingjiang.github.io/blog/post/exploration/) and, more importantly, novel questions.
