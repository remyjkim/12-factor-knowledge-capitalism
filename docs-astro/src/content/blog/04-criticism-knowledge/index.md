:::brief
- Conjectures are guesses. They earn the status of knowledge only by withstanding severe attempts at refutation.
- The quality mark of an explanation is that it is *hard to vary* — every part does load-bearing work, so it cannot be quietly patched to fit any outcome.
- Criticism is the system's quality regulator: without it, unfalsifiable and endlessly patched explanations silently fill the knowledge base with noise.
:::

## The claim

A conjecture is not yet knowledge. However elegant the guess, it earns that status only by surviving criticism. This is Popper's engine: knowledge advances through bold conjectures subjected to severe attempts at refutation — we attack our best guesses as hard as we can, and what withstands is what we provisionally keep.[^1] The word *provisionally* is doing real work. Nothing that survives today is exempt tomorrow; survival is a status, not a certificate.

## The mechanism

What separates the survivors from the casualties? Deutsch's criterion: a good explanation is *hard to vary*.[^2] Every part of it does load-bearing work, so you cannot quietly adjust a component to accommodate an unwelcome observation without the whole structure failing. Bad explanations are the opposite — *easy to vary*. Rescue one with an ad-hoc amendment and it survives anything, which means it explains everything, which means it explains nothing.

Verifiability is the gate on the funnel. A claim that forbids no observation cannot be corrected by any observation, and a system that admits unfalsifiable, endlessly patched explanations silently fills with noise — its knowledge base grows while its knowledge does not.

## Why it matters

Criticism is the regulator of quality for the entire framework. Exploration proposes; criticism disposes; and the loop of the two is the evolution interface — the process by which a civilization's stock of explanations improves without anyone decreeing which are true. It keeps the body of knowledge honest, discarding what cannot be tested and hardening what can. And occasionally criticism does its most dramatic work: forcing a paradigm shift that does not merely replace an answer but reframes the questions themselves.[^3]

The stakes recur at every scale of this framework. The institutions of Factor 11 are, at bottom, machinery for keeping this loop running at civilization scale — and the noise that unfalsifiable claims inject is exactly what the market of Factor 10 must pay verification costs to filter.
