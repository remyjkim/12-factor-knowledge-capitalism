Knowledge is not mere information but information that enables effective action. [Information theory](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf) formalizes information as the reduction of uncertainty, but explicitly notes that semantic aspects are irrelevant to the engineering problem. [Cybernetics](https://uberty.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Norbert_Wiener_Cybernetics.pdf) sought something deeper, the negative entropy of order that maintains organization. The distinction matters, because all knowledge is information but not all information is knowledge. A random bitstream has high Shannon information yet teaches nothing. A signal that updates your model of reality and improves your decisions is knowledge. The [information value theory](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSSC.1966.300074) makes this precise. Information has worth only insofar as it changes what you would do. Agents under incomplete state information [reduce uncertainty through observation](https://lup.lub.lu.se/search/files/5323668/8867085.pdf), a framework later developed into [partially observable Markov decision processes](https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf). Life, organizations, and civilizations are fundamentally sequential decision-making systems. Their objective is survival, the maintenance of organization against entropy, and they pursue it by accumulating knowledge that improves their capacity to act under uncertainty.
